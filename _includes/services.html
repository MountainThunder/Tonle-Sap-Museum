Fishing Village Tour

the largest floating and stilt village on Cambodia's Tonle Sap Lake, located about 55km east of Siem Reap. Home to over 10,000 residents, it features massive houses built on stilts up to 10 meters high. It offers a highly authentic, off-the-beaten-path cultural experience.

Lotus Farm Experinece

Lotus flowers are immensely important in Campbodian life. Lotus is the national flower and a symbol of resilience, purity, and spiritual awakening. Because it emerges untouched from murky waters, it holds deep spiritual significance in both Hindu and Buddhist traditions.

Traditional Khmer Cooking Class

Experience traditional khmer food and the joy of cooking how Khmer people in this village have cooked for generations. An authentic traditional Khmer cooking class is an immersive, cultural journey that transforms fresh farm and market ingredients in an open-air kitchen. It emphasizes the fresh, rustic flavors of Cambodia, teaching you how to balance the elements of sweet, sour, salty, and umami.
