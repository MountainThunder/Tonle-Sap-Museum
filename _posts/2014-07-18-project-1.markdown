Kampong Khleang is the largest floating and stilt village on Cambodia's Tonle Sap Lake, located about 55km east of Siem Reap. Home to over 10,000 residents, it features massive houses built on stilts up to 10 meters high. It offers a highly authentic, off-the-beaten-path cultural experience.
