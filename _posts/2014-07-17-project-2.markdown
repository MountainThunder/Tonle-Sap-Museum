Lotus flowers are immensely important in Cambodian life. Lotus is the national flower and a symbol of resilience, purity, and spiritual awakening. Because it emerges untouched from murky waters, it holds deep spiritual significance in both Hindu and Buddhist traditions.

The lotus flower has been cultivated and gathered wild for thousands of years. Learn about how every part of the plant is used for food, traditional medicine, textiles and as a spiritual offering at temples and shrines.

Walk through a working lotus farm and see these beautiful flowers up close. Try a delicious fresh lotus seed right out of a freshly picked lotus head.
